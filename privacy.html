Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-20

youtube-brain is used only by dac studio for its own YouTube channels. It is not offered to the public.

Data accessed

With read-only permissions (youtube.readonly, yt-analytics.readonly), the tool reads channel and video statistics and YouTube Analytics reports of channels owned by dac studio. This tool uses YouTube API Services; see the Google Privacy Policy at https://policies.google.com/privacy.

How data is used and stored

Data is used only to produce internal reports. It is stored in a private repository, is not sold, and is not shared with third parties.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact

Use the support email shown on the Google consent screen.
